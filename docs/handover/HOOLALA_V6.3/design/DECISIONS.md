Accepted by the owner on Sat 31 Oct 2026. Do not reopen; log any conflict in the checks report and ask. All v6.2 decisions still stand unless changed here.

## Accepted in v6.3

- **The design must not change during the build.** Copy, layout, sizes, chips, controls and timings are built as drawn. A deviation needs the owner's OK.
- The question step opens with **HELLO, HUMAN. Five quick ones, then you're out of here.**
- **Five questions, owner's copy**: last lost track of time (with a follow-up), who you said more than hi to (with a follow-up on who you only nodded at), battery check, what you nearly said, what to keep off the table.
- **Mostly chips, voice always available**: one shared voice component with a new chips slot and reply line.
- **Hoolala! replies to some chips** (CAN'T REMEMBER and the four battery levels), with the owner's exact lines.
- **Processing happens once at the end**: Muse Spark removes filler and keeps only the bits useful for small talk and kindness tries. No per-answer AI line.
- **Snake while you wait**: starts by itself, arrow keys and WASD on desktop, inverted-T buttons on mobile, walls wrap, OOPS. GO AGAIN., each food reveals a kept bit. No score of any kind.
- **FULL battery puts START HERE on BOLD.** (Changes the v6.2 rule "never BOLD".)
- **CAN'T REMEMBER always starts on SOFT**, overriding the battery.
- **What you nearly said becomes a BOLD KIND IT try.**
- **Off-table answers filter tries**: FAMILY removes family members from tries, WORK removes colleagues.
- People are tiered: **said more than hi** (BRAVE and BOLD) and **only nodded at** (SOFT).

# Decisions · HOOLALA v6.1

Accepted by the owner. Do not reopen; log any conflict in the checks report and ask.

## Accepted in v6.1
- Softkeys are TODAY · AROUND · FRIENDS, three keys, on every top-level screen. Short flows (Surprise me) use LEAVE and a contextual right key.
- The header is the brand only (HOOLALA! and the tagline); no status bar, signal or battery icons, no screen names.
- Around me starts with the radar visible and idle; the scan runs in place. The main key sits in the thumb zone. The live privacy line is kept word for word.
- The radius starts at 500 m and only widens by the person's tap, up to 2 km.
- The talking rating is about places, never people; it is asked once on leaving, and shown only from 3 ratings.
- Menus and events come only from the place's own website or public page, with the source shown. No scraping of map or review sites.
- Fit (GOOD / OK / LOW) and the talking rating are computed by code. Muse Spark only writes words (WHY GO, READ IT) and must cite the facts it used.
- The compass shows four directions (MORE OF, TALK ABOUT, LESS OF, HOW I GO) over the five stored parts. Every line shows its source. Nothing is saved without the person's confirmation; every change can be undone.
- Compass information comes from four routes only: Surprise me, FIND ME, say it your way, and the COMPASS? row after a try.
- Surprise me is answered by holding a pixel mic in the thumb zone; the ball is Hoolala!'s face, not a button. Captions are always on; SOUND controls speech only.
- The typed lines and the radar sweep are must-keeps.

## Rejected (do not reintroduce)
- Two equal-weight keys on the Around me first screen.
- Status bar with signal and battery in the header.
- Holding the ball itself to talk (moved to the thumb-zone mic).
- Widening the search radius automatically.
- Star ratings or ratings of people.

## Rule changes approved
- Places may carry a rating (talking here), as an aggregate about the place only. People are never rated (rule 41 unchanged).

## Still open (owner)
- Around me scope: Option A (Around me in full, Friends = dare link only) or Option B (Friends as planned, Around me without menus and ratings).
- Places provider: reuse FIND ME's provider unless decided otherwise.
